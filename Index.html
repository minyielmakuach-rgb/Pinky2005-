About Us

Ngok Lual Yak Students' Association at Universities & Higher Institutes (NLYSAUHI) is a non-profit student organization based in Juba, South Sudan (Konyokonyo Market).


We bring together all Ngok Lual Yak students across universities and higher institutes to promote unity, academic support, welfare assistance, and community development.


Our Motto is "Education is Light."

🎯 Mission

Foster unity, support academic excellence, and provide welfare assistance to needy students.

👁️ Vision

An educated, united, and empowered generation leading community development.

🤝 Values

Unity, Education, Service, Integrity, and Culture.
